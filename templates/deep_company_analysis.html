Analysis Results

mdna_sentiment
Average tone of the management discussion.
An average over every scored sentence, so one glowing paragraph barely moves it. A high value means the optimism is sustained.
outlook_sentiment
Tone of the section read as the outlook.
Today it carries the same value as mdna_sentiment. Treat it as a placeholder until the forward sentences are scored on their own.
forward_ratio
Share of sentences about the future.
High means the section is spent on what is coming rather than on what happened. It says nothing about tone: a company can be forward-looking and gloomy at once.
hedge_ratio
Share of sentences full of hedging words.
"May", "could", "we believe" everywhere means the claims are being softened. Good news wrapped in hedging is weaker than the tone alone suggests.
financial_sentences
How many sentences survived the filters.
This is the sample size, not a score. A tone built on twenty sentences is far shakier than one built on two hundred.
