Sentiment Ranking Fallback

Ask the ranking bot about the management discussion of a portfolio's filings
The five numbers behind every ranking Name any of them in the query to rank by it
mdna_sentiment
Average tone of the management discussion.
Positive means the wording leans optimistic across the whole section. It is an average, so one glowing paragraph does not move it much: a high value means the optimism is sustained.
outlook_sentiment
Tone of the section read as the outlook.
Today it carries the same value as mdna_sentiment, so ranking by one or the other gives the same order. Treat it as a placeholder until the forward sentences are scored apart.
forward_ratio
Share of sentences that talk about the future.
High means management spends the section on what is coming rather than on what happened. It says nothing about tone: a company can be forward-looking and gloomy at the same time.
hedge_ratio
Share of sentences full of hedging words.
High means "may", "could", "we believe" everywhere: the claims are being softened. Read it next to the tone, because good news wrapped in hedging is weaker than the tone alone suggests.
financial_sentences
How many sentences survived the filters and got scored.
This is the sample size, not a score. A tone built on twenty sentences is far shakier than one built on two hundred, so check it before trusting an extreme ranking.
